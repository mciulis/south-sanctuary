# South Sanctuary Moving Sale — Emails

Five emails total: 3 sent manually by Mike (Announcement, First Look, Next in Line) and 2 sent automatically by the website when a new reservation is submitted (Auto-Confirm Top Pick, Auto-Confirm Waitlisted).

**Placeholders to fill in before sending the manual ones:**
- `[Name]` — recipient first name
- `[Item]` / `[Item 1]`, `[Item 2]` — item name(s)
- `[PRICE]` — sale price per item
- `[AMOUNT]` — deposit per item (25% of sale price, rounded to nearest $10, min $10)
- `[TOTAL]` — sum of all deposits for that person
- `[N]` — waitlist position number
- Venmo handle: `@mciulis` — attach Mike's Venmo QR Code PDF where noted

**Pickup details:**
- Scheduled waitlist pickup: Friday May 22 or Saturday May 23
